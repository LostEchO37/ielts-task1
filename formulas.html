小作文动态图公式段（开头 + 结尾）

注意介词准确性

建议整体格式

开头段
↓
结尾段（总览）
↓
主体段
批注：
  • 段间空一行美观
  • ☆☆ 为避免/减轻时间不够的残文扣分程度（先写总览段）

· 开头段（1 句，别废话）

公式：

The [图（照抄题干）] illustrates the changes in sth. (per + 单位) + 地点 + over a span of xx years.

（附）常考同替：

类别同替
种类kind / type / category
百分比percentage / proportion（常用）
花费spending = expenditure on = cost of
每年annual sth. = sth. per year

例：

The table illustrates the changes in the amount of milk produced per year in four countries over a span of 20 years.

· 结尾段

公式：

Overall, it can be observed from the data that [趋势总结]. It is also evident that [最值].
占位符写法
趋势总结 most + 主语 + 都 + 趋势, with the exception of + 例外项, which + 趋势
最值 若无最值，则写于（时间）+ 主语 + surpass / overtake + 对象 + 成为最…

例：

Overall, it can be observed from the data that most nations' amount of milk produced showed an upward trend, with the exception of Australia, whose figure declined. It is also evident that Netherlands' quantity of milk produced remained the highest, while Guatemala's figure remained at a minimum level.
