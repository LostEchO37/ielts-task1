静态图公式段（适配静态特征）

框架参考动态图，但开头与总结须按静态图改写，勿照搬「changes in / 趋势总结」。

☆ 另注： 静态图可沿用动态图的开头段 & 总结段框架，但须适配：开头写差异/对比（differences between），总结写占比或最大差值（静态图无趋势）。整文结构依然推荐先写开头（一段）+ 总结（一段），再写主体段以求保险。

→ 对照动态图公式段完整笔记

· 开头段（静态图适配）

公式：

The [图（照抄题干）] illustrates/shows/compares the differences between [对比项] (per + 单位) + 地点 + (in + 年份/时间点，如有).
勿用动态图写法： 静态图不要写 the changes in … over a span of xx years（除非题干明确给出时间跨度）。

（附）常考同替：

类别同替
种类kind / type / category
百分比percentage / proportion（常用）
对比differences between / comparison of / breakdown of

例：

The bar chart shows the percentage of transport modes in City X.
The pie chart compares the differences between various energy sources in Country Y in 2022.
The table illustrates the amount of milk produced in four countries.

· 总结段（Overall）

框架（与动态图相同）：

Overall, it can be observed from the data that … It is also evident that …

占位符写法（静态图专用 — 无趋势总结）：

占位符静态图写法
占比总结 most + 主语 + account for the largest/smallest proportion / share, with … taking up xx%
最大差值 the gap / difference between X and Y is the most significant / X is nearly double that of Y
勿写趋势： 静态图 Overall 不要写 showed an upward trend / with the exception of … which declined 等动态趋势句。

例（bar chart · 占比）：

Overall, it can be observed from the data that car is the most popular transport mode, accounting for the largest proportion at 45%. It is also evident that rail has the smallest share, taking up only 5%.

例（table · 最大差值）：

Overall, it can be observed from the data that Netherlands produces the most milk among the four countries. It is also evident that the gap between the highest and lowest figures is the most significant, with Netherlands nearly double that of Guatemala.
