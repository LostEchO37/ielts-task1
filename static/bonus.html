小作文静态图 · 五大加分点

☆ 注意掌握如何在句中自然带出具体数据

一、主语多样性

照抄 + 同替 + 万能 + 数字 — 与动态图一致。

批注（红）： 由于无时间变化，无法使用时间句大法（The xx-year period witnessed…）。其余四项参考动态图。

→ 动态图主语多样性详解

二、相似

A and B shared similar / comparable figures in sth., at xx and xx respectively.
There is (was) a marked similarity in sth. between A and B, at …

三、相差 & 倍数

A ~, compared with ~ in/for B
A ~, xx percentage points higher/lower than that (of) B
批注（红）： that (of) B 的介词要与前面保持一致（没有 a）
A ~, which is (double / triple / quadruple / xx times) that (of) B

quadruple = 四倍

四、段间衔接（万能公式法，动态图也适用）

段落公式
主 1 段One of the prominent features is that …
主 2 段In terms of xxx, it is apparent that …
主 3 段What also stands out is that …

五、占比

  • ~~~ at xx%
  • ~~~, with the figure standing at xx%
  • （荐） ~~~, taking up xx% (of ~)
