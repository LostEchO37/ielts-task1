例题 · 2.1.1

（政府）发展太空科技 VS 其他

按「先舍 +（更好 & 不足）× 2」完整走一遍审题、立场与三个主体段。

审题四步

步骤内容
类型两个议论词（太空科技 vs 其他领域）
限定无额外范围词；注意主体是政府（不是个人）
立场一边倒 — 支持「其他领域」比太空科技更重要
结构先舍，更好 & 不足 × 2

立场句（Thesis）

While there is a perception among many people that governments should spend money on space technology, I would argue that other fields can play a more prominent role.

主 1 段 · 先舍（让步太空科技）

段首句：

Undeniably, there are significant benefits to investing in space technology.

思路链

环节展开
背景 grapple with numerous challenges — overpopulation, environmental degradation, energy shortages
条件 若政府投资探索外太空 → 也许发现 habitable planets + alternative resources
因此 seemingly address the urgent issues of humans
功能： 承认太空科技有道理，为后文转折做铺垫。

主 2 段 · 医疗优先

段首句（转折）：

Nevertheless, while the advantages of space technology are acknowledged, it is crucial to note that other priorities are more related to human life, especially when it comes to tackling medical issues.

思路链

环节展开
条件 投资 → 建 hospitals / vaccine research / training of medical professionals → diseases be cured
结论 lifespan → extended
驳对方 太空 — have little relevance to humans + cannot deliver immediate benefits

主 3 段 · 教育优先

段首句：

In addition, the importance of education cannot be overlooked.

思路链

环节展开
背景 educational inequality — disadvantaged areas, lack qualified teachers / basic teaching facilities
条件 If 投资 (train teachers / donate equipment) → financial aid → receive proper education
结论 the illiteracy rate ↓ → more productive workforce
驳对方 太空 — consume a great deal of time & money + short-term benefits 少

高分词汇与语法

本篇例题提炼 — 按功能分类，可直接套用到同类「A vs B」比较题。

立场 · 让步

表达用途
While there is a perception that …引出现象 / 他人观点
I would argue that …明确一边倒立场
play a more prominent role强调某方更重要
Undeniably, there are significant benefits to …主1 · 先舍让步
Nevertheless, while … are acknowledged, it is crucial to note that …主2/3 · 转折收束
In addition, … cannot be overlooked主3 · 追加论点

背景 · 问题

表达用途
grapple with numerous challenges宏观背景
overpopulation / environmental degradation / energy shortages具体挑战并列
educational inequality社会不平等
disadvantaged areas弱势群体 / 地区
when it comes to tackling medical issues限定讨论领域

论证 · 结果

表达用途
habitable planets / alternative resources太空投资潜在收益
address the urgent issues of humans总结积极面
vaccine research / training of medical professionals医疗投资具体措施
diseases be cured → lifespan extended条件 → 结果链
financial aid → receive proper education教育支持链条
the illiteracy rate ↓ → a more productive workforce数据变化 → 宏观效益

驳斥对方

表达用途
have little relevance to humans指摘对方脱离民生
cannot deliver immediate benefits短期效益不足
consume a great deal of time & money成本过高
short-term benefits are limited短期回报少
句式模板： 背景 → If / 投资 + 具体措施 → 结果（↓ / ↑ / be cured）→ 驳斥对方（while 太空 …）
